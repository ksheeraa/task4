# 📊 Sales & Financial Analysis Dashboard (Task 4)

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze financial sales performance across various European markets between 2019 and 2020.

## 🚀 Project Goal
The objective of this project was to transform raw sales exports into actionable insights. By building a centralized dashboard, we can identify which countries, categories, and managers are driving the most profit.

## 📁 Dataset
- **Source:** `Sales-Export_2019-2020.csv`
- **Scale:** €113.36M in Total Sales
- **Key Metrics:** Sales (EUR), Cost, Profit, and Date.

## 🛠️ Key Features
* **KPI Scoreboard:** Instant view of Total Sales, Total Profit, and Sales Growth.
* **Time-Series Analysis:** A line chart showing monthly sales trends to identify seasonality.
* **Regional Performance:** Comparative analysis of sales across Sweden, Finland, Portugal, and Spain.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Users can filter the entire report by **Country** or **Product Category**.
* **Consistent Theme:** A professional, color-coded design for high readability.

## 📈 Dashboard Insights
* **Growth:** Achieved a **0.22% growth** rate in 2020 compared to 2019.
* **Top Category:** **Clothing** is the highest-grossing category at over €17.6M.
* **Efficiency:** Maintained a healthy profit margin of approximately **16.7%**.

## 💻 Tech Stack
* **Power BI** (Visualization & Dashboarding)
* **Power Query** (Data Cleaning & ETL)
* **DAX** (Custom Financial Measures)

## 🔧 Installation & Usage
1. Download the `Sales_Analysis.pbix` file.
2. Open with **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the slicers on the side to interact with the data!
